## Objective

Freeze the smallest coherent S2 implementation that lets the founder use **editable synthetic business forms** in one browser:

company/service → lead and discovery → versioned proposal → simulated founder review and rework → delivery plan/tasks → invoice/receivable drafts → follow-up and today overview.

The result must support changing synthetic inputs and revising/reviewing proposals. A second fixed four-action fixture does not satisfy S2.

Preserve the accepted S1 physical leaf and existing legacy UI. S2 contains no real model calls, real owner authorization, real customer data, external business actions, or real payment assertions. S3-M, S3-L and final MVP integration remain required for the complete goal.

## Required interface decisions

The design candidate must settle the following with exact fields, enums, visibility, function signatures, routes, limits, error semantics and named tests. These are architect decisions, not choices left to Luna.

1. **Experimental surface and startup.** Identify the independent S2 command, module/package boundary, assets and routes. Show its call/dependency graph and why neither S1 `run(u16)` nor legacy `ui` changes behavior. Prefer existing frontend/Rust capabilities where compatible; identify helpers that would drag in ambient store, identity or effect setup.

2. **Synthetic editing boundary.** Enumerate editable company, service, relationship, discovery and proposal fields; field lengths, value bounds and allowed operations. Freeze one synthetic seed and editable variations, provenance labels, memory lifetime, reload/restart/reset semantics and limits. State exactly how the interface communicates synthetic-only use. A warning is not proof that arbitrary entered text is synthetic. No model/egress or protection claims follow from the label.

3. **Canonical business state.** Define company/service, relationship/contact, opportunity, discovery, evidence, assumption, clarification, proposal version, review, engagement, milestone/task, invoice draft, receivable, simulated payment and follow-up relationships. Define IDs, revision allocation, missing references, deletion/reset and transition atomicity. Keep one authoritative experiment state; projections and task results are derived.

4. **Evidence and fact provenance.** Distinguish seeded source facts, founder-entered synthetic assertions, assumptions and unresolved questions. A button, generated draft or stage change cannot fabricate customer confirmation. Freeze source-reference validation and readable provenance/history.

5. **Money and proposal versions.** Freeze currency scope, integer minor units, input parsing, quantity/rounding/overflow, totals and tax exclusion/unknown handling. No inferred actual taxes or exchange rates. Define immutable proposal snapshots, predecessor relations, permitted revisions, stale-write detection, differences and invalidation of prior review after changes.

6. **Review and rework.** Freeze draft/submitted/returned/rejected/accepted simulation transitions and exact reviewed-version binding. Rework creates a new version; stale or duplicate actions have defined backend results. Founder business review is explicitly simulated and cannot produce a capability, OwnerSession or send authorization.

7. **Delivery and billing.** Engagement binds the accepted proposal version. Scope changes require an explicit revised business reference and review path. Tasks, milestones and acceptance drafts cannot silently become completed/customer-accepted. Define projected value, accepted-order value, draft invoice, simulated issued receivable, simulated payment and remaining balance independently; reject currency mismatch, overpayment and invalid references.

8. **Browser interaction.** Freeze routes and DTOs for today, company/service, opportunities, customer/case detail, proposal/review, delivery and billing. State command ordering, request/version preconditions, HTTP validation, failure behavior and safe rendering. Demonstrate the complete case without manual JSON or copying between pages. Include empty, validation-error, stale revision, returned proposal, interrupted response and reset behavior.

9. **Five-role S3 seams.** Identify extension points for analyst, quotation assistant, delivery planner, quality checker and legal assistant: immutable input snapshot/reference, role/skill version, task status, provenance, result binding and draft-application boundary. S2 may display “not connected”; it cannot pretend seeded output is a real model result. Actual TaskSpec/TaskResult/ActionProposal and network contracts remain subject to S3-00/S3-M review; no empty orchestration framework is required in S2.

10. **Legal/RAG seams.** Preserve unknown and multiple jurisdiction facts, including SG, EU/member state and US/state; do not infer applicable law. Identify how later reports/rules bind proposal version, fact snapshot and source/rule versions, show citations and stale/insufficient/uncovered states, and block prohibited experiment transitions at the backend. Actual retrieval, model review and rule implementations belong to S3-L; S2 must not fabricate legal approval.

11. **Reuse and dependencies.** For each reused operation/helper, specify the concrete call site and authority/storage side effects. Where direct reuse is unsuitable, explain the boundary and scope of the minimal replacement. Freeze exact dependency/features/lockfile changes if any; no generic registry, second workflow engine, custom authorization layer or speculative database.

12. **Tests and delivery.** Every implementation card names positive and rejection tests, exact commands and execution-count checks. Freeze the real-browser founder checklist, screenshots/evidence paths, release launch command and S2 acceptance report requirements. Keep measurement scripts in Git before producing reported numbers.

## Boundary decisions and unresolved dependencies
